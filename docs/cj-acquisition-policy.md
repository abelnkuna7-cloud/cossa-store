# Qualification-first CJ acquisition policy

A supplier item may enter the Cossa Store working catalogue only when the sourcing workflow can establish enough evidence to justify further commercial review.

Required evidence includes lawful/supported category, useful customer proposition, live supplier inventory, available variants, South Africa fulfilment feasibility, landed-cost visibility and a price path that protects Cossa Store from unsustainable selling.

Discovery alone is not stock. A draft is not an approved product. Provider uncertainty is not a commercial rejection. Public status is granted only after the commercial qualification stage succeeds.

Bulk acquisition stays paused until the pre-qualification engine enforces these rules before draft creation.
